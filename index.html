The gentle part matters

Guide the fruit. Let the plant lead.

We make the process simple enough for a weekend gardener, while leaving plenty of room for the slow magic of growing.

01

Choose your moment

Wait until a young, healthy fruit has set. Your kit’s guide shows exactly what to look for before you begin.

02

Clip on the mould

Position the food-safe guide gently around the fruit. Air, light, and water still do their important work.

03

Watch the reveal

Check in as it fills out, then lift away the mould at harvest for a fruit-shaped little celebration.

No pressure, no force — just a supportive shape guide at the right time.
